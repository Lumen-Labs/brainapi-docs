# Public Shared Brains billing proposal

## Recommendation

Keep Stripe purchases and financial settlement in the MaaS Next.js server. Treat the listing owner or owner workspace as the beneficiary and the consumer's authorized workspace as the payer. Qualify only a completed query against a live public listing whose debit consumed verified purchased funds. Apply one versioned platform rate in integer basis points to eligible USD micros at execution. Persist the effective rate, beneficiary, listing and brain IDs, payer team, credential ID, funding allocations, and stable operation ID. Aggregate fractional minor units before transfer and carry the remainder. Exclude free allowance, platform grants, unknown-origin credits, non-query operations, and same-beneficiary self-usage from earnings. Decide paid subscription and top-up allocation explicitly before counting either.

### Funding and settlement

1. Add an idempotent purchase record keyed by Stripe PaymentIntent and fulfill from authoritative successful payment state. Use a stable purchase-request ID at Stripe creation and funnel synchronous responses, async SCA, webhook delivery, and repair into a single fulfillment transaction. Record currency, actually received principal, charge and payment references, and original lot balance. Preserve balances for legacy customers while keeping unknown origin separately classified. Backfill eligible lots only from reconciled payment and spend history.
2. Use one transactional allocator for the existing wallet, with all debit writers participating and one compatibility rule for legacy, grants, and verified paid lots. Existing ingestion reservations and cycle/retry charges must consume lot balances consistently or the revenue feature can assign already-spent funds a second time. A separate paid-only pool is viable if existing wallet migration is too risky, but requires an explicit customer funding choice and a policy on whether these credits are usable elsewhere. Establish one precedence policy with product before selecting included units versus paid debit. A metering outage blocks new paid operations.
3. Reserve before executing and transition a unique operation from reserved to settled or released. Scope client idempotency keys to payer, listing, credential, operation and payload fingerprint. Persist claim and outcome so parallel retries cannot run the same reservation twice or allow one debit to serve unlimited upstream calls. Establish timeout/expiry and replay semantics for disconnects, upstream success with a lost response, and application crashes. A known failure releases the same allocations. An ambiguous outcome remains in reconciliation until resolved. Use an explicit lease/fencing strategy so a stale worker cannot settle a reclaimed reservation. Enforce public visibility on new admission and make in-flight settlement policy explicit. Commit prepaid usage and append-only owner earnings in the same transaction as finalization. Count billable retrieval once, with no additional owner earnings for the lower-level calls that serve the same operation. Generalize prepaid usage calculation so monthly invoicing cannot debit already-paid queries again.
4. Reconcile payment refunds and disputes by charge. Remove or freeze affected unspent lots and append adjustments for the attributable spent amount. Keep refunded credits from being spent again and use owner payable offsets for previously settled earnings when reversal is unavailable. Use unique event IDs and object/state deduplication. Refund and dispute handlers must be order-independent and derive monetary facts from Stripe rather than webhook order.

### Stripe Connect

Use hosted onboarding with the account configuration approved for supported countries and platform loss responsibilities. Store connected account linkage server-side and derive onboarding and payout readiness from verified Stripe state. Generate expiring Account Links from an authorized owner session. A redirect is insufficient evidence of completed onboarding. Refresh the account on return and process Connect account changes. See [Stripe-hosted onboarding](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/hosted-onboarding).

Keep the existing customer payment on the platform and move the owner share later via separate Connect transfers. Create a durable settlement batch per beneficiary and currency with immutable allocated rows, amount, destination, and idempotency key. Use a transactional outbox and compare-and-set worker lease, map each transfer to its batch, and reconcile with Stripe before repeating an ambiguous attempt after the idempotency window. Gate transfers on approved account capabilities, funds availability, a hold period and minimum, and the platform's supported country/currency corridor. Keep separate owner statuses for accrued, held, available, transferred to Stripe, and bank payout when Stripe can substantiate it. A connected-account payout may combine transfers, so report it separately from per-brain earnings. [Separate charges and transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers) · [Stripe idempotency](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests) · [Connected-account payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/payouts-bank-accounts).

Track the platform's liquidity and residual refund exposure after transfers. A Stripe transfer reversal is a separate action and can be unavailable when the connected balance is insufficient. Keep the local adjustment and reconciliation path independent of Stripe recovery. Verify signatures from raw request bodies and durably enqueue processing before acknowledgement. [Stripe transfer reversals](https://docs.stripe.com/api/transfer_reversals) · [Stripe webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks).

## Alternatives

- Transfer on every query. This would make sub-cent payments impossible and create external side effects on the query path.
- Transfer at credit purchase using destination charges. At purchase time, future destinations and spend amounts are unknown, and credits can be used on multiple brains or other products.
- Compute earnings from existing query telemetry or catalogue price. Query rows show usage and attribution but do not establish whether that usage was actually funded by purchased credits.
- Include subscriptions and paid query add-ons. This may increase eligible traffic, but requires a separate allocation contract for multi-feature subscriptions and expiring query units. Recoverable source funding and refund treatment must be specified.

## Assumptions and open decisions

- The initial money currency is USD, consistent with current credit purchases. Confirm Connect platform country, eligible owner countries, onboarding configuration, payout hold, cadence and threshold before enabling real transfers.
- Choose percentage, effective-date policy, tax/fee treatment, and contract terms before live accrual. A clear MVP candidate is a platform-wide rate on principal paid for credits, excluding tax and reducing the principal for refunds. Percentage changes apply prospectively.
- Confirm whether the user means cash packs, period query packs, paid plan allowances, or any combination. Decide allowance precedence and behavior at exhaustion, including whether a separately consented wallet-funded query can run after a plan's published usage pause. The current Ask plan advertises 30,000 included queries for Shared Brains.
- Confirm billable units across surfaces. One successful explicitly metered retrieval is a tractable unit. If browser Ask participates, define whether one answer or each successfully served retrieval counts and handle streaming terminal state. Keep Explore and auxiliary retrievals free unless explicitly repriced.
- Confirm the beneficiary identity, authorization, account replacement, treatment of earnings while onboarding is incomplete, owner transfers, and unpublishing. A historical earning keeps its original beneficiary.
- Whether a connected account can receive transfers or pay out depends on platform and connected account configuration. Confirm the intended integration in Stripe test mode with launch corridors before production.

## Risks and validation

- Simultaneous API/MCP queries, ingestion, and invoice cycle against one low balance cannot overdraw or consume the same proven funds. Verify on a transaction-enabled test database.
- One successful PaymentIntent fulfills once across sync, webhook, retries, and SCA completion. Failed payments or refunds delivered early cannot become eligible funding.
- Test false payer, wrong scope, spoofed listing and rate, owner self-usage, team-only/unpublished access, allowlisted read-only calls, exhausted balance, and a lost membership. All should prevent inappropriate debit or earning.
- Test retries before and after commit, same key/different payload, crash after reserve, upstream failure, stale lease, replayed Stripe event, ambiguous transfer result past Stripe key expiry, insufficient platform balance, deauthorized account, failed payout, partial refund, and lost dispute.
- Reconcile lot principal with remaining funds, reservations, spend and refunds. Reconcile net owner earnings with held/available balances, settlements, carry and reversals. Verify cents conversion across many microcharges.
- Exercise existing free/paid allowances, plan usage limits, ingestion wallet spend, and ordinary private/team operations before enabling the public path. Prove new prepaid queries appear once in usage and create no second charge.
