# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project. Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on the work, and respect privacy and differing levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sexualized conduct in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owners through GitHub. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence, consider context and impact, and take proportionate action. Retaliation against a reporter is not tolerated.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and public spaces where someone represents the project.
